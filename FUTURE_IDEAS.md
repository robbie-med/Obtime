## Postpartum (deliberately left out of the 2026-09 timeline overhaul)

Extend the timeline past birth (weeks 0–12 postpartum, then to the child's first birthday).
Candidate items — each needs the same source check as the pregnancy timeline before it ships:

- **US:** postpartum contact within 3 weeks and a comprehensive visit by 12 weeks (ACOG);
  postpartum depression screening; contraception; follow-up glucose test after gestational
  diabetes (4–12 weeks); blood-pressure checks after hypertensive disorders; newborn visits,
  hepatitis B birth dose, and nirsevimab for the baby if the mother had no RSV vaccine.
- **Korea:** 산후조리원 stay (booking is already on the pregnancy timeline); 출생신고 within
  1 month; 부모급여 / 아동수당 / 첫만남이용권 applications; 산후조리경비 (local, e.g. Seoul);
  newborn screening and the national infant vaccination schedule; 영유아 건강검진.
- **Crossover in reverse:** registering a Korean-born baby with a US consulate (CRBA),
  passports, and carrying records back to US care.
- **Nutrition after birth:** 미역국 and iodine (limit ~2 bowls/day per MFDS — already noted in
  the nutrition guide), breastfeeding needs (DHA, vitamin D for the baby).
